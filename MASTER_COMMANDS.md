## 🎬 3. MEDIA, VIDEO & AUDIO TRANSCODING (FFMPEG & YT-DLP)

### High-Speed Video Processing
```zsh
# Transcode raw video to compressed web-optimized H.264 (CRF 23)
ffmpeg -i input.mov -vcodec libx264 -crf 23 -preset fast -acodec aac output.mp4

# Convert 16:9 widescreen video to 9:16 vertical TikTok/Short with center crop
ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf "scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920" -c:a copy short.mp4

# Slice lossless clip between 00:00:30 and 00:01:30 (instant copy, no re-encoding)
ffmpeg -ss 00:00:30 -to 00:01:30 -i input.mp4 -c copy clip.mp4

# Strip video and extract lossless WAV audio
ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vn -acodec pcm_s16le output.wav

# Generate high-resolution optimized GIF from video
ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf "fps=15,scale=480:-1:flags=lanczos,split[s0][s1];[s0]palettegen[p];[s1][p]paletteuse" out.gif

# Concatenate multiple video files without re-encoding
ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i filelist.txt -c copy final_merged.mp4
```

### Mass Image Processing & Optimization

# Batch convert all PNGs to modern WebP (Quality 85%)
